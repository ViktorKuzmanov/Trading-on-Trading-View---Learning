## 2. Strategy: Supply & Demand

- I trade **Supply and Demand**
- **Support and Resistance** is almost the same as Supply and Demand
- Key skill: **how to mark zones**

### Zone patterns

- **Base** — when price moves sideways in the same place for a while
- **Rally, Base, Drop** — price goes up, pauses (base), then drops → supply zone
- **Pullback** — when price comes down after being at a high price

### Zone rules

- Zones (the base rectangles I draw) must **not be bigger than 150 pips**

## 6. Session 2 — Zone Quality & Structure

> Short explanations below are the general Supply & Demand meaning of each term, written to match what was said in the session. Items marked **(confirm with Fendy)** were unclear in my notes.

### Strong point — "Are all criteria met?"

- A zone is a **strong point** only when **all the criteria are met**
- Criteria covered in this session: **fresh zone**, **BOS**, **sideways (base)**, **momentum** (confirm with Fendy that this is the full list)
- If even one is missing → it is not a strong point, skip it

### Fresh zone (cycle not complete)

- A zone price has **left and not come back to yet**
- The **cycle is not complete** — the zone hasn't been tested, so the orders there are still waiting
- Once price returns and reacts, the cycle is complete and the zone is no longer fresh

### Sideways (base)

- Price moving **sideways** = the **base** (same as in section 2)
- This is where the zone rectangle is drawn

### Momentum (video ~9:13)

- How **strongly** price leaves the base — big, fast candles in one direction
- Strong momentum out of the zone = stronger zone

### BOS — Break of Structure (video ~9:16)

- A BOS **indicates a better zone**
- Example (sell): the **right price is lower than the left price** → the new low (right) breaks below the previous low (left)
- The zone that caused the break is the one to look at

### Flip ("it's complicated")

- **Instead of a sell, go for a buy** (and the other way around)
- A zone that gets broken can flip its role: broken supply can act as demand
- Mentor said this part is complicated — learn it later in more depth

### Change of character (CHoCH) — just to know it

- Said "just so you know it" — awareness only, not a core rule yet
- General meaning: the **first break against the current trend**, an early sign the trend may be changing (BOS = trend continues; CHoCH = trend may reverse)

### M1 timeframe — preference is yours

- Using the **1 minute (M1)** chart is **my own preference** (confirm with Fendy what M1 is used for — e.g. entries)
