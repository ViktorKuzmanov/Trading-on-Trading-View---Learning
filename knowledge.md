## 2. Strategy: Supply & Demand

- I trade **Supply and Demand**
- **Support and Resistance** is almost the same as Supply and Demand
- Key skill: **how to mark zones**

### Zone patterns

- **Base** — when price moves sideways in the same place for a while
- **Rally, Base, Drop** — price goes up, pauses (base), then drops → supply zone
- **Pullback** — when price comes down after being at a high price

### Zone rules

- Zones (the base rectangles I draw) must **not be bigger than 150 pips**

## 3. Risk & Targets

- **SL = Stop Loss**
- Target on live: **profit 40 pips**

## 4. Pips for Gold

- Look at the last numbers of the price
- Example: if the price shows **95.200**, that is **9520 pips**
- (rule: drop the decimal point and the last digit → 95.200 → 9520)
