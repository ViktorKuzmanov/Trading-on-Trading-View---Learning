02 · Strategy

Supply & Demand trading

Support & Resistance≈Supply & Demand

Supply zone

Like resistance — area above price where sellers stepped in. Price tends to drop from here.

Demand zone

Like support — area below price where buyers stepped in. Price tends to rally from here.

Main skill to master: how to mark zones correctly.

03 · Zones

What is a base?

  • Price moves sideways and stays in the same place for a while
  • Small candles, no clear direction
  • This is the area I draw the rectangle around
03 · Zones

Rally → Base → Drop

Price goes up, pauses in a base, then drops hard. The base becomes a supply zone.

04 · Rules

Marking zones — rules

≤ 150

pips max for any zone (base rectangle) I draw

  • Look on the 5 minute interval
  • Find the base
  • Draw the rectangle around it
  • Too big (>150 pips)? Not a valid zone
