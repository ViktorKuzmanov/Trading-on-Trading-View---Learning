02 · Strategy

Supply & Demand trading

Support & Resistance≈Supply & Demand

Supply zone

Like resistance — area above price where sellers stepped in. Price tends to drop from here.

Demand zone

Like support — area below price where buyers stepped in. Price tends to rally from here.

Main skill to master: how to mark zones correctly.

03 · Zones

What is a base?

  • Price moves sideways and stays in the same place for a while
  • Small candles, no clear direction
  • This is the area I draw the rectangle around
03 · Zones

Rally → Base → Drop

Price goes up, pauses in a base, then drops hard. The base becomes a supply zone.

Session 2 · Zone quality

Fresh zone

  • Price left the zone and has not come back yet
  • The cycle is not complete — orders are still waiting there
  • Once price returns & reacts, the zone is no longer fresh
Session 2 · Zone quality

Momentum

  • How strongly price leaves the base
  • Big, fast candles in one direction = stronger zone
  • Slow, weak move out = weaker zone

Rewatch the session video at ~9:13.

Session 2 · Structure

BOS — Break of Structure

  • A BOS indicates a better zone
  • Sell example: the right price is lower than the left price
  • The zone that caused the break is the one to watch

Video at ~9:16

Session 2 · Advanced

Flip

  • Instead of a sell, go for a buy
  • A broken zone can switch roles: old supply acts as demand

"It's complicated" — learn this in more depth later.

Session 2 · Good to know

Just so I know it

Change of Character (CHoCH)

The first break against the current trend — an early sign the trend may be turning. BOS = trend continues, CHoCH = trend may reverse. Awareness only for now.

M1 timeframe
